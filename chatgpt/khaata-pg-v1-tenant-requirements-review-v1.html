Tenant Requirements Domain v1

This closes the data-model gap intentionally left visible in the previous read-model build.

Frozen behavior preserved: each requirement is Required, Optional, or Not collected. Missing a required item does not block tenant creation; the tenancy may remain Setup incomplete and be followed up operationally.
Authority stays separate: whether staff may proceed despite missing requirements is still handled by Staff & Access permission/approval policy, not by the requirement record itself.

Storage model

tenant_requirement_policies stores the business policy. tenancy_requirement_items stores what a specific tenancy has provided. A derived view computes setup_incomplete and missing-required count.

Read model integration

business requirement policy ↓ Required / Optional / Not collected ↓ tenancy_requirement_items ↓ v_tenancy_requirement_status ↓ missing required? ├─ no → no attention noise └─ yes → Setup incomplete → actionable Home attention where relevant

Supported initial requirement categories

Phone number · Identity document · Emergency contact · Photo · Address/basic details · Other documents.

Service API

setPolicy() updates business expectations. markProvided() records tenant data/document completion. getStatus() returns Setup incomplete and missing-required counts without inventing blocking behavior.

This layer can now be wired into Add Tenant, Tenant Detail and Home attention without violating the rule that real-world occupancy may continue while documentation is incomplete.
