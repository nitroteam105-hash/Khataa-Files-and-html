PG V1 Product Flow

Structural review map. This is not a visual-design specification. Tap a lifecycle phase to inspect the owner journey, major actions and where the existing V1 screens fit.

✓ Covered△ Review＋ Potential gap
Create PG business

Establish the business shell and PG/Hostel capability.

Business creation → PG setup
Set up capacity

Rooms only, floors & rooms, or blocks/buildings → floors → rooms → beds. Setup may be skipped and completed progressively.

Property setup → Rooms & Beds
Add tenant

Basics → Room/Bed → Money → Requirements → Review. Missing required details may save as Setup incomplete.

Tenants → Add tenant → Tenant detail
Future joining

The same tenancy can remain Upcoming until the agreed joining date. No-show does not silently cancel or release the bed.

Tenant record

Identity, room/bed, agreement terms, security held, current financial state, requirements and recent payments.

Tenant list → Tenant detail
Room transfer

Move the occupant while preserving occupancy history. Rent changes only when explicitly chosen.

Agreement changes

Due-day and responsibility changes are prospective/effective-dated rather than rewriting history.

Monthly rent obligation

Active rent agreement deterministically creates the monthly obligation. Current and older dues remain distinguishable.

Collections → Due / Needs attention
Record payment

Actual money received is recorded and applied to obligations. Partial payments remain part-paid rather than creating a replacement balance.

Collections / Tenant → Record payment → Receipt
Exceptions

Overdue, missed promise, dispute, reversal, refund, waiver or correction become explicit financial events/states.

Statement

Ledger projection for the tenancy; security remains separate.

Tenant detail → Statement
Add charge

Electricity or other tenant charges stay distinct from rent and from payments.

Add expense

Business-paid expense or staff-pocket reimbursement; optional property context.

Cash custody

Cash collected by staff settles the tenant payment immediately but creates physical custody with that staff member.

More → Cash custody → Handover
Cash handover

Expected versus actual cash is confirmed; discrepancies require explicit resolution.

Plan move-out

Record intended move-out and notice. This creates planned vacancy but does not yet end the tenancy.

Tenant detail → Plan move-out
Change / cancel plan

Extension, changed date or cancellation updates the future plan without corrupting occupancy history.

Exit settlement

Final dues, explicit security application, refund due and bed release are handled separately.

Moving out → Exit settlement
End tenancy

Moves the lifecycle to Former. Outstanding receivables can survive the move-out; they are not deleted.

Pending refund / old dues

Financial follow-up remains actionable after physical departure until resolved or explicitly written off.

Staff & permissions

Owner, manager, caretaker/front desk and accounts with allowed / approval / not allowed decisions.

More / Settings

Property, billing, requirements, cash custody, expenses, optional electricity/food, business settings and backup/sync.

Structural check

✓ Core lifecycle is covered

Setup → tenant → occupancy → billing → payment → departure → former tenant has a coherent path.

△ Needs dedicated V1 treatment

Room transfer, payment correction/reversal, security refund pending, dispute/write-off, and approval request are defined in the model but do not yet have dedicated structural screens.

＋ Likely missing review surface

A former-tenant financial follow-up view may be useful so old dues or pending refunds remain discoverable after the tenant leaves.
