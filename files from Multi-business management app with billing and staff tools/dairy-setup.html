Business modes, delivery plans & retail items

Business modes
Home delivery
Daily/recurring delivery, billed monthly
Walk-in retail
Over-the-counter sales, billed per visit

At least one mode should be on. Both can run together — many dairies do daily delivery routes and also sell over the counter.

Delivery products

Set quantity, unit, and price per day — the monthly estimate is calculated for you automatically, using the actual days in the current month. Skip-day credits also come from this same daily rate, so it stays consistent everywhere.

Retail items (rate list)

Simple one-off pricing — no subscription, billed via the Retail bill layout per transaction.
