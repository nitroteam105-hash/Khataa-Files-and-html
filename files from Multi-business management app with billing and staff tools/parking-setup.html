At least one mode should be on. Many parking operators run both — reserved monthly slots plus open walk-in spots.

Monthly pass rates (by vehicle type)

Same Fee Plans used across the app — one monthly rate per vehicle type. A customer picks their vehicle type and cycle when they sign up for a slot.

Walk-in rates (by vehicle type)

Simple one-off pricing per hour or per day — no reservation, billed via the Retail bill layout per visit.
