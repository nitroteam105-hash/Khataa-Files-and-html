Business modes & delivery products

Business modes
Home delivery
Daily or specific-day delivery, billed monthly
Walk-in retail
Stall/counter sales, billed per visit
Ad leaflet insertion
Local businesses pay to insert flyers into deliveries — B2B income

At least one mode should be on. Many newspaper vendors run all three — a delivery round, a stall, and ad insertions as a side income.

Delivery products

Set quantity, unit, frequency, and price per occurrence — the monthly estimate is calculated for you. Daily items use the actual days in the month; weekly items (e.g. "Every Sunday") use the actual count of that weekday in the month, since some months have 4 and some have 5.

Retail items (rate list)

Simple one-off pricing — no subscription, billed via the Retail bill layout per transaction.

Ad insertion rate card

Set a price per copy for each leaflet size — the total for any booking scales naturally with how many copies the advertiser wants (e.g. 1,000 copies vs 2,000 copies), rather than one flat fee regardless of quantity.
