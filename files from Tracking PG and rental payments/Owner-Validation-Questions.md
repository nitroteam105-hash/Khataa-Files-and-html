# Owner Validation Questions

Questions to ask real small-business owners (PG, gym, tiffin, coaching, shop, etc.) before building. Goal: find out if the problem is real and if they'd actually use and pay for a solution — **without leading them to say yes.**

**How to use this:**
- Pick 5–10 owners across different business types
- Keep it conversational, not a survey — let them talk
- **Listen more than you pitch.** Don't sell; learn.
- Watch for *emotion* — frustration is a buying signal; politeness ("nice idea") is not
- Note their exact words

---

## PART 1 — Understand how they work today
*(Don't mention your app yet. Just learn their reality.)*

1. How do you currently keep track of who has paid you and who hasn't?

2. Show me — what do you actually use? (notebook / Excel / WhatsApp / memory / an app?)

3. How much time do you spend each month on collecting payments and following up?

4. What's the most frustrating part of managing the money side of your business?

5. Has anyone ever paid late, not paid, or disputed a payment? What happened?

6. Do you handle cash, UPI, or both? Roughly what split?

7. How do you keep records of your staff — who worked how many days, their salary?

8. Where do you keep important documents (IDs, agreements)?

---

## PART 2 — Find the real pain
*(Dig into what actually hurts.)*

9. In the last 3 months, did you lose money or time because of a tracking/record mistake? Tell me about it.

10. If you could wave a magic wand and fix ONE thing about running your business admin, what would it be?

11. Have you ever forgotten to collect a payment, or had someone's plan/rent quietly lapse without you noticing?

12. Do you run more than one business or location? How do you manage them together?

---

## PART 3 — Test current solutions
*(Learn what they've tried and why it didn't stick.)*

13. Have you ever tried an app or software for this? Which one?

14. If yes — are you still using it? Why / why not?

15. If you stopped, what made you go back to the old way?

16. What would an app need to do for you to actually switch from your notebook/WhatsApp?

---

## PART 4 — Show the prototype
*(Now show your screens — home dashboard, money in/out, ledger. Watch their face.)*

17. (Show home screen) What do you think this is? What would you do here?
*(If they can't tell — that's important feedback on clarity.)*

18. (Show money in/out) Is this how you think about your money? Would this be useful?

19. What's missing that you'd absolutely need?

20. What here is confusing or unnecessary?

21. Would your staff / your customers be able to use their part of this?

---

## PART 5 — The money question
*(The most important answers. Don't soften these.)*

22. If this app saved you the hassle you described, would you use it?

23. Would you pay for it? *(Wait. Let them answer. Silence is fine.)*

24. If yes — how much per month feels fair to you?

25. What would make it NOT worth paying for?

26. Would you recommend it to other owners you know? Who?

---

## PART 6 — Wrap up

27. Can I come back and show you the next version?

28. Do you know 2–3 other owners I should talk to?

---

## What to listen for (signals)

**Strong buying signals:**
- They get *animated/frustrated* describing the problem
- They've already tried (and paid for) solutions
- They ask "when can I get it?" unprompted
- They give a specific price without hesitating
- They immediately name other owners to talk to

**Weak / warning signals:**
- "Nice idea" / "good app" (polite, not committed)
- "I'd use it if it were free" (no real value perceived)
- They can't describe a specific recent pain
- They're happy with their notebook and see no problem
- Vague "maybe" on paying

**The honest test:** if owners aren't *already frustrated* and aren't willing to *name a price*, the pain may not be sharp enough — better to learn that now than after building.

---

## Quick version (if you only have 5 minutes)

If an owner is busy, ask just these four:
1. How do you track who's paid and who hasn't today?
2. What's the most frustrating part of that?
3. (Show one screen) Would this help? What's missing?
4. Would you pay for it — and how much feels fair?
